# Beckett Hackathon Video Teleprompter Script

Target pace: calm, clear, and slightly faster than normal conversation. Aim for `2:50` to `2:58` total. The paragraph labeled `Easy trim point` and the mini reply-drafting example can be shortened if the final edit runs long.

## Script With Timing

### `0:00-0:22` - Problem Hook

On screen: Slack thread with Jordan's vague message already open.

Voiceover:

> Workplace communication is full of hidden meaning. A message like, "Can you take another pass at this before Friday? I want reviewers to understand the value faster," might be simple for one person, but stressful and confusing for someone who relies on clearer context. Is this urgent? Are they annoyed? What exactly needs to change?

### `0:22-0:30` - Product Intro

On screen: `title-card.svg`, or the same title text as an overlay on Slack.

Voiceover:

> Beckett for Slack is a private workplace communication coach that helps neurodivergent workers decode messages, draft replies, and prepare for difficult conversations directly inside Slack.

### `0:30-1:06` - Demo 1: Decode A Vague Message

On screen: Slack. Open the message shortcut and choose the live Beckett action that works best: `Beckett - Decode`, `Beckett - Respond`, or the current equivalent label.

Voiceover:

> Here, I am looking at a vague message from a manager before a one-on-one. Instead of guessing, I can use Beckett from the message itself.
>
> Beckett separates what is actually visible from what might only be an interpretation. It does not claim my manager is annoyed if the message does not show that. Instead, it explains the likely ask, names what is uncertain, and gives me practical reply options I can choose from.

### `1:06-1:35` - Mini Demo: Draft A Calm Reply

On screen: Slack. Use the same thread or Beckett assistant conversation and ask:

```text
/beckett respond Help me reply to Jordan without sounding defensive. I want to ask what he means by "understand the value faster."
```

Voiceover:

> Beckett can also help turn that read into language I can actually use. Here, I ask it to help me respond without sounding defensive.
>
> Instead of writing one generic answer, Beckett gives me a few options with different tones, so I can choose what fits the relationship and the moment.

### `1:35-1:45` - Why This Matters

On screen: Keep Beckett's reply options visible.

Voiceover:

> That matters because the goal is not to replace the user's judgment. The goal is to make the invisible parts of communication easier to see.

Optional add-on, easy trim point:

> Slack is a useful place for Beckett because so much workplace communication now happens here, but Slack removes many of the context clues people use in person, like tone of voice, facial expression, body language, and timing. Beckett is designed for that gap. It helps users slow down, check the evidence, and respond clearly without assuming hidden intent.

### `1:45-2:42` - Demo 2: Prep For A Difficult Conversation

On screen: Slack. Type and run:

```text
/beckett prep I need to talk to my manager about workload in my 1:1
```

Voiceover:

> Beckett can also help with higher-stakes conversations. I can run slash Beckett prep and ask for help preparing to talk to my manager about workload.
>
> Instead of giving one giant wall of advice, Beckett starts a private guided coaching flow. It asks focused questions, helps organize what I want to say, and can use relevant Slack context when available.
>
> The result is concrete: an opening line, talking points, likely pushback, and a follow-up message. Everything stays private unless I choose to copy or send something.

### `2:42-2:56` - Personal Why

On screen: keep the final Beckett output visible. Optional small text overlay: `Private by default. User stays in control.`

Voiceover:

> I built Beckett because I am a former special education teacher. I worked with students who were capable and thoughtful, but who often needed explicit support with tone, indirect language, and hidden expectations. After high school, those supports often disappear, even though many of those same people enter the workforce.

### `2:56-2:59` - Close

On screen: `closing-card.svg`.

Voiceover:

> Beckett helps neurodivergent workers communicate clearly inside the tools where work already happens.
